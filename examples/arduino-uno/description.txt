Board: Arduino Uno (ATmega328P), 5V logic, 16 MHz crystal.

Sensors:
- DHT22 temperature/humidity sensor on digital pin D2 (single-wire protocol)
- HC-SR04 ultrasonic distance sensor: trigger on D9, echo on D10
- LDR photoresistor (light level) on analog pin A0

No I2C or SPI devices in this build. One hardware UART used only for USB serial
debug output at 9600 baud. No RTC, no external watchdog (use internal watchdog
timer). Battery-free, USB-powered only, so no low-power/sleep requirements.

Goal: read all three sensors every 2 seconds and print readings over serial.
